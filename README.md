## What I'm building

**[WeOrda](https://weorda.com)**, digital compliance for transportation. Drivers complete inspections, boarding, and drop-off checks from a mobile app; operators, districts, and commissioners get a real-time, audit-ready record. Live on iOS and Android, piloted with industry partners. First pilot: 98% on-time reports, up from about 65% on paper. [Case study](https://www.svetlana-burninova.link/weorda.html)

**[YPA Finance](https://www.ypa.finance)**, personal finance technology in the user's own language. Budgeting, a predictive payment calendar, credit tools, and multilingual AI assistants across 13 languages, with Plaid, Equifax, and Stripe integrations. Live on iOS and Android, 5.0 on the App Store. [Case study](https://www.svetlana-burninova.link/ypa-finance.html)

## How I work

- **Security first.** Zero Trust, multi-tenant isolation, encryption at rest and in transit, and audit logging designed in from the start. I ran a real Zero Trust migration and [wrote up what the vendors do not tell you](https://hackernoon.com/we-thought-zero-trust-would-take-3-months-six-months-in-were-still-migrating).
- **Cost is a design input.** A multi-architecture EKS cluster with Karpenter [cut production compute costs by 70%](https://hackernoon.com/how-i-cut-aws-compute-costs-by-70percent-with-a-multi-arch-eks-cluster-and-karpenter).
- **Infrastructure as code, always.** Terraform across AWS, GCP, and Azure, with CI/CD and zero-downtime deployments.
